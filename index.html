Lab for Computational Genomics

School of Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

Hi, welcome! Our lab focuses on developing algorithmic methods for managing, storing and analyzing large genomic data, and gaining biological insight from these data. In particular, we are interested in algorithmic problems occurring in the following areas.

  • Reconstruction of focal amplifications and complex structural variations in cancer, including extrachromosomal DNA (ecDNA);
  • Genomic data compression/indexing;
  • Metagenomics, pangenomics and cancer microbiome;
  • 3D genomics/chromatin structure analysis;
  • Secure and privacy-preserving genomic data analysis.

We are recruiting! We are actively looking for postdocs, PhD and Master’s students (2027), undergraduate research interns, with backgrounds in Computer Science, Mathematics, Bioinformatics, or Statistics. If you are interested, please send your CV to kaiyuanzhu@sjtu.edu.cn.

To prospective students: Computational biology/genomics is a fascinating yet challenging field. Many times there is a precise computational problem behind the questions biologists ask (which is often well hidden and waiting to be discovered). What we do is first uncover that problem, i.e., identify what to compute (this is usually the most critical step), and then work out how to guarantee that the results are correct (or provably close to optimal) and how to compute them efficiently.
